### What does Thyra do?

Thyra transforms how you interact with DeFi by combining three powerful components into one seamless flow: an AI Strategist, a Visual Strategy Engine, and a Self-Custody Vault. Each is powerful on its own—together, they unlock a new level of effortless and secure automation.

* **The AI Strategist** Think of it as your personal DeFi analyst. Describe your goal—anything from “diversify my airdrop tokens” to “auto compound my LP yields.” Thyra’s AI doesn’t just generate strategies — it analyzes your wallet, understands protocol mechanics, and simulates outcomes before you commit. It helps you make smarter moves, not just automated ones.
* **The Visual Strategy Engine** Build your strategy like puzzle pieces with our Blockly-powered drag-and-drop interface. Assemble logic, conditions, and actions intuitively—no code, no complexity. Every workflow is pre-registered on-chain for full transparency.
* **The Self-Custody Account** Your keys, your coins. Strategies execute from your own secure wallet. No one else can move your assets—ever.

How they work together:

1. **You Describe.** Tell the AI Strategist what you want—e.g., “DCA into BTC and ETH weekly” or “Take profit when my portfolio grows 20%.” It designs a strategy tailored to you.
2. **You Build & Approve.** Review, tweak, and connect blocks in the Visual Engine. See your strategy come together like a flowchart. Every action is pre-registered on-chain. You see what will happen — before it happens.
3. **Thyra Executes.** The Engine runs your strategy automatically. It triggers swaps, harvests yield, and manages risk — all from your Self-Custody Wallet. You stay in control; Thyra does the work.
4. **You Adapt & Grow.** Monitor everything on one dashboard. Visually adjust, duplicate, or sunset strategies in seconds. Run multiple strategies across multiple chains—with clarity and confidence.

Thyra brings together AI, visual automation, and self-custody so you can deploy sophisticated DeFi strategies—without code, complexity, or compromise.

### What makes Thyra different

Decentralized Finance (DeFi) promises to bring financial freedom to users, yet its implementation path still suffers from fragmentation issues and is time-consuming and labor-intensive. There are three widely adopted solutions available on the market:

* **Automated Tools** Represented by rule-based automated bots, they execute fixed tasks (such as arbitrage, rebalancing, profit-taking, and stop-loss) based on preset conditions. However, they cannot generate strategies nor operate independently. They are like a basic coffee maker: it can brew a pre-set cup, but it can't discover new beans, adjust the grind for you, or learn that you prefer a stronger blend on Mondays. Their rigidity leaves users to do the hardest part: figuring out _what_ strategy to run in the first place.
* **Intent Copilots** A new breed of AI assistants that are great at conversation and can suggest general ideas or simulate trades. But the conversation often ends where the real work begins. They are like a brilliant navigator who can describe the scenic route in detail but can't actually drive the car. Critically, most rely on the protocol holding your private keys to execute — forcing you to trade true ownership for automation. These tools lack a truly self-custodial execution layer, reintroducing not just complexity, but significant custodial risk.
* **Strategy Engines** Powerful, programmable platforms that offer immense flexibility for experts. Yet, they come with a steep learning curve, requiring deep knowledge of smart contracts and financial engineering. They are the equivalent of handing someone a blank spreadsheet and a book of formulas to build their own trading model—powerful in the right hands, but utterly overwhelming for most. They democratize _access_ to tools, but not _expertise_.

In general, most current tools on the market can only cover part of the workflow, forcing users to integrate all links by themselves. This is why Decentralized Finance (DeFi) and Decentralized Artificial Intelligence Finance (DeFAI) still feel difficult to use to this day.
